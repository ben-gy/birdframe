# Artwork attribution

The plates in `plates/` are cut from John Gould's *The Birds of Australia*
(1840-48), lithographed by Elizabeth Gould and H.C. Richter. The lithographs
themselves are long out of copyright.

**But most of these scans are not public domain.** 34 of the 36 are Rawpixel's
digitally restored facsimiles, published on Wikimedia Commons under
**CC BY-SA 4.0**. Rawpixel licenses the restoration work even though the
underlying lithograph is free.

That has two consequences:

1. **`plates/` is CC BY-SA 4.0, not MIT.** The code in this repository is MIT;
   the artwork is not, and cannot be relicensed. Anything derived from these
   files must stay share-alike and carry this attribution.
2. Each file below names its source, author and licence, as CC BY-SA requires.

Upstream fugleramme ships its `classic` style under the same licence, so this
is the normal situation for this artwork rather than a quirk of this set.

To avoid share-alike entirely, re-source from the original scans at the
[Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/105698),
which are public domain - at the cost of dirtier, unrestored plates.

## Notes

- **Australian Wood Duck** keeps its background: Gould drew the birds on water
  among reeds, and that scenery is the artwork rather than the paper. It will
  read as a rectangle in a collage.
- **Grey Butcherbird** sits on pink-tinted paper inside a printed frame and
  needed a looser tolerance than the rest to cut cleanly.
